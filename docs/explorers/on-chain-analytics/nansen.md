# Nansen

**Nansen** is a commercial on-chain analytics platform that labels blockchain addresses with real-world entities ("whales", "exchange wallets", "smart money", etc.) and combines on-chain signals with market data. It is used for research into flows, token movements, and investor behavior.

## What it is

- A **subscription-based** analytics service for Ethereum and several other chains.
- **Proprietary** — closed-source, paid tiers.
- Provides dashboard metrics, alerts, and entity-labeled wallet data.

## Key features

- **Wallet labels** — aggregation of addresses into known entities and categories.
- **TokenGod / Nansen portfolio** — per-token holder and flow breakdowns.
- **Heatmaps & market apps** — visual overviews of activity and trends.
- **Alerts** — real-time notifications for tracked wallet activity.
- **Nansen Research** — published insights.

## Use cases

Fundamental research on token distribution, fee-monitoring, "smart money" identification. Useful for risk teams and analysts; reliance on labels means accuracy depends on Nansen's classification.

> **License:** Proprietary — not open source.
