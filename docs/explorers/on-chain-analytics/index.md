# On-chain analytics

On-chain analytics platforms index blockchain data and transform it into charts, dashboards, and derived metrics for market, protocol, and wallet research. They are powerful for engineers, analysts, and researchers — not investment advisors.

## Categories

- [Dune Analytics]({{ 'explorers/on-chain-analytics/dune' | relative_url }}) — SQL-based querying of decoded on-chain data.
- [Nansen]({{ 'explorers/on-chain-analytics/nansen' | relative_url }}) — labeled wallets and "smart money" signals.
