# Explorers and analytics

Tools for viewing and analyzing blockchain data: **block explorers** let you look up blocks, transactions, addresses, and tokens; **on-chain analytics** platforms add dashboards, metrics, and derived insights over raw chain data.

## Domains

- [Block explorers]({{ 'explorers/block-explorers' | relative_url }}) — Blockstream.info, Etherscan, Blockchair.
- [On-chain analytics]({{ 'explorers/on-chain-analytics' | relative_url }}) — Dune Analytics, Nansen, and similar.
