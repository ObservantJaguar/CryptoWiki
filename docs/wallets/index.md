# Wallets

Wallet products and services for storing keys and managing on-chain assets. They are categorized by custody model: **hardware** (offline), **software** (local/hot), and **custodial** (third-party held).

## Domains

- [Hardware wallets]({{ 'wallets/hardware' | relative_url }}) — Ledger, Trezor, ColdCard.
- [Software wallets]({{ 'wallets/software' | relative_url }}) — MetaMask, Electrum, Exodus.
- [Custody]({{ 'wallets/custody' | relative_url }}) — custodial exchanges and institutional custody.
