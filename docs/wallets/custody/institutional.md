# Institutional custody

**Institutional custody** providers are specialized services for funds, companies, and high-net-worth holders that need professional-grade key management, regulatory compliance, and insurance-grade security. They differ from retail exchanges in offering dedicated, often segregated storage.

## What they offer

- **Secure key management** — hardware security modules (HSM), multi-party computation (MPC), or multi-signature wallets.
- **Segregated/on-chain custody** — separate wallets or account structures per client.
- **Compliance** — KYC/AML, audit, and reporting to satisfy regulators and counterparties.
- **Insurance** — coverage against theft/hacks, a key differentiator from retail exchanges.
- **Staking/DeFi access** under custody agreements.

## Notable providers

- **Coinbase Custody / Prime** — regulated US custody. https://www.coinbase.com/prime
- **BitGo** — MPC-based custody and wallet infrastructure. https://www.bitgo.com
- **Fireblocks** — MPCC/HSM wallet infrastructure and custody. https://www.fireblocks.com
- **Copper** — UK-based, institutional custody. https://copper.co
- **Anchorage Digital** — chartered digital-asset bank. https://www.anchorage.com

> These are **proprietary** services.

## Key trade-off

Institutional custody reduces operational risk and provides compliance, but still entails counterparty trust. It contrasts with fully self-custodied setups favored by the non-custodial philosophy elsewhere in this wiki.
