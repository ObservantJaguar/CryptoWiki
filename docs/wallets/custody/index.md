# Custody

**Custody** refers to arrangements where a third party — an exchange, bank, or specialized custodian — holds private keys (or manages assets) on behalf of the user. The user controls an *account*, not the keys themselves.

## Key distinction

- **Self-custody** — you hold the private keys/seed (hardware/software wallets). Only you can move funds.
- **Custodial** — the service holds the keys. It can freeze, censor, or lose assets; you rely on its solvency and security.

## Why it matters

- Custodial services simplify onboarding and support, taxes, and compliance — but introduce **counterparty risk**.
- The phrase "not your keys, not your coins" captures the trade-off.
- Regulated custodians (e.g. licenced exchanges) are subject to KYC/AML and special duties.
