# DeFi instruments

**Decentralized Finance (DeFi)** replaces traditional financial intermediaries with smart contracts. This domain catalogs the core instrument types and their flagship implementations: DEX/AMM engines, protocol aggregators, and stablecoin protocols.

## Domains

- [DEX and AMM]({{ 'defi/dex' | relative_url }}) — Uniswap, Curve, and automated market makers.
- [Aggregation and routing]({{ 'defi/aggregation' | relative_url }}) — 1inch, Paraswap, and aggregator tooling.
- [Stablecoins]({{ 'defi/stablecoins' | relative_url }}) — DAI, USDT/USDC, and pegged-asset protocols.
