# Stablecoins

**Stablecoins** are cryptocurrencies designed to maintain a stable value, usually pegged to a fiat currency (most commonly the US dollar). They are the liquidity backbone of DeFi, giving users a low-volatility unit of account on-chain.

## Types

- **Fiat-collateralized** — backed 1:1 by fiat reserves (USDT, USDC).
- **Crypto-collateralized** — overcollateralized by volatile crypto (DAI).
- **Algorithmic** — supply-adjusted without direct collateral (historically riskier).
