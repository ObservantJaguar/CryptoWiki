# Staking and mining

This domain covers the software and services behind two ways of securing a network: **staking** (locking assets on PoS chains) and **mining** (computational work on PoW chains).

## Domains

- [Staking]({{ 'staking-mining/staking' | relative_url }}) — validator software, staking services, and liquid staking.
- [Mining]({{ 'staking-mining/mining' | relative_url }}) — mining software and pools for PoW networks.
