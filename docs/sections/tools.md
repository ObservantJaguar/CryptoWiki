# Tools

An encyclopedia of protocols, node software, wallets, explorer services, DeFi instruments, and developer tooling relevant to blockchain engineering. Every tool or protocol has its own page describing what it is, its architecture, and links to official documentation.

## Domains

- [Node software]({{ '/nodes' | relative_url }}) — protocol implementations by network: Bitcoin, Ethereum, and alternative networks.
- [Wallets]({{ '/wallets' | relative_url }}) — hardware, software, and custodial wallet products.
- [Explorers and analytics]({{ '/explorers' | relative_url }}) — block explorers and on-chain analytics services.
- [DeFi instruments]({{ '/defi' | relative_url }}) — DEX/AMM engines, aggregators, and stablecoin protocols.
- [Developer tools]({{ '/dev-tools' | relative_url }}) — smart-contract frameworks, libraries, and node APIs.
- [Staking and mining]({{ '/staking-mining' | relative_url }}) — staking services, validators, and mining software.
