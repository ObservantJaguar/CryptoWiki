# Theory

The theory section covers the foundational concepts behind cryptocurrencies, blockchains, and decentralized systems. Each domain contains category pages explaining a family of related ideas, plus in-depth program pages where a specific algorithm or standard merits one.

## Domains

- [Blockchain fundamentals]({{ '/theory/fundamentals' | relative_url }}) — blocks, hashing, Merkle trees, and how a chain is formed.
- [Consensus mechanisms]({{ '/theory/consensus' | relative_url }}) — PoW, PoS, DPoS, PoA, and BFT-family protocols.
- [Cryptography in crypto]({{ '/theory/cryptography' | relative_url }}) — public-key crypto, signatures, hash functions.
- [Wallets and keys]({{ '/theory/wallets-keys' | relative_url }}) — key custody, seed phrases, address derivation standards.
- [History]({{ '/theory/history' | relative_url }}) — from cypherpunk e-cash to Bitcoin, Ethereum, DeFi, and NFT.
- [Regulation basics]({{ '/theory/regulation' | relative_url }}) — legal regimes, KYC/AML (neutral, non-legal notes).
